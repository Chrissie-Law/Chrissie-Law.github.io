I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at [KTL Lab](https://ktl.buaa.edu.cn), School of Artificial Intelligence, Beihang University, advised by Prof. [Fuzhen Zhuang](https://fuzhenzhuang.github.io). 

My research focuses on Recommender Systems. Specifically, I work on:

- ⭐ **Causal Inference for Recommendation.** Integrating causal inference techniques (e.g., counterfactual reasoning, causal structure modeling) into recommendation models to mitigate spurious correlations and improve accuracy, robustness, and interpretability.

- 🌐 **Multi-Domain Recommendation.** Designing knowledge-transfer mechanisms for platforms with multiple exposure scenarios to reduce development and maintenance costs and improve performance.

- 🧩 **Multi-Task Recommendation.** Developing cross-task interactions and conflict-mitigation mechanisms to avoid negative transfer and achieve better overall performance when optimizing multiple objectives (e.g., click, dwell time, conversion).

- 🎯 **Feature Selection for Recommendation.** Developing algorithms to identify essential signals and suppress redundancies among large numbers of input features in large-scale recommender systems.
